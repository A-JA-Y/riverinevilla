This piece compares the realistic alternatives on the terms that separate them, rather than on amenity lists that all read the same.

### Where Riverine differentiates

**Township scale.** 85 acres with a separately registered apartment precinct, commercial at the gates and 19 acres of reserved open space is a different proposition from a 15- or 20-acre villa enclave. It means the social infrastructure is inside the boundary rather than a drive away.

**Retained landscape.** Around 4,000 trees across 100 to 120 species, and a protected riparian corridor that the master plan was built around rather than over. Most competing projects landscape after clearing; this one retains.

**Density.** 217 villas on roughly 50 acres — fewer than 4.5 per acre. Check this number on every project you shortlist, because it is the one that cannot be retrofitted.

### Where the alternatives win

**Possession timing.** Embassy Riverine has a RERA-filed completion date of 30 September 2032. Several alternatives are delivered or near-delivered. If you want to move in within two years, Riverine is the wrong answer regardless of its merits.

**Entry price.** Rs 14.10 crore is the floor at Riverine. Most alternatives start meaningfully lower, and for a buyer whose priority is a villa rather than a township, that gap buys a lot.

**Established social infrastructure.** Yelahanka and Jakkur have mature neighbourhoods around them today. Tarahunise is earlier in that curve.

## How to decide
<a id="decide"></a>
Four questions, in order:

1. **When do you actually need to move in?** This eliminates more options than anything else. A 2032 RERA date is disqualifying for some buyers and irrelevant to others.
2. **What is the density?** Ask for villas-per-acre, not "acres of greenery". The second number is marketing; the first is structural.
3. **What is the all-in cost?** Base price plus GST, stamp duty, registration and deposits. On a Rs 14.10 crore villa that is roughly Rs 1.6 crore of additional cost before deposits.
4. **Is the landscape retained or planted?** Retained canopy is mature on day one. Planted landscape takes a decade.

<Callout type="note">
Visit at your own commute hour, not at 11am on a Sunday. Drive times on NH-44 vary materially, and the brochure figure is not the figure you will live with.
</Callout>
